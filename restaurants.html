Member story

An evening on board the British Pullman for The Supper Club

Few settings in the world combine history, motion, and culinary artistry quite like the British Pullman. For one remarkable evening, Quintessentially arranged a private takeover of two of the train's storied carriages for an intimate group of members.

The five-course menu was created by Michelin-starred chef Nieves Barragán Mohacho, served with Dom Pérignon 2013 as the locomotive glided through the English countryside at dusk — the golden light of the dining car matching the champagne in every glass.

It was, by all accounts, one of those evenings that becomes a story told for years. Quintessentially arranged every detail: the guest list, the carriage décor, the chef's brief, the wine selection, and the seamless transfers before and after.
