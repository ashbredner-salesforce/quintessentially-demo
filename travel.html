Member story

A three-week journey through South Asia

When a family of five approached us with a dream — three weeks, three countries, and a wish to experience the real South Asia — our travel team set to work crafting something truly extraordinary.

The trip took them through Bhutan (where through our Virtuoso partner status we arranged a complimentary Bhutanese cultural guide and secured the last room at the property of the decade), on to Cambodia for a private sunrise at Angkor Wat, and finally to the palaces and backstreets of India.

At every hotel — each one the best in its category — the family arrived to personalised welcome amenities, upgrades, and staff who knew their names. Not because we asked. Because we have the relationships.
